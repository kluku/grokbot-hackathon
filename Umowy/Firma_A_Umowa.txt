4. Equipment Storage Requirements.

4.1. Secured Storage Space; Secure Container. Contractor will provide a secure, lockable location for the storage of the Equipment in the Authorized Location (“Secured Storage Space”). The Secured Storage Space must be a separate, secured room, cabinet or safe. Without limiting any specific requirements set forth herein, Contractor will ensure that the Equipment is secured with three layers of lockable security during storage (“Three Layer Security Requirement”). In addition, if necessary to achieve the Three Layer Security Requirement, Contractor will additionally provide a secure, lockable cabinet within the Secured Storage Space that is more than a file cabinet but less than a safe (“Secure Container”). For example, Contractor may store the Equipment within the Secure Container, within the Secured Storage Space, within the secured Authorized Location, or may store the Equipment within the Secured Storage Space, within a Work Space with an access control system, within the secured Authorized Location.

4.2. Storage Requirements and Process. The Equipment must be stored in the Secured Storage Space (and within the Secure Container, if necessary to fulfill the Three Layer Security Requirement) during periods of non-use and non-working hours.
